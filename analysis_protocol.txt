Revised analysis specification, 28 October 2026

The original specification dated 14 October 2026 is preserved at source_history/analysis_protocol_original.txt. The study is exploratory and was not preregistered. This revision retains the original outcome, observations, predictor definitions, primary validation and model grids, and adds the reviewer-requested analyses below together with a supplementary exploratory CatBoost comparison.

Outcome and sample
Retain the ten original categories of Q 23, including no need and hard to say, as a nominal outcome. The question concerns the greatest perceived barrier to wider AI use and does not measure a personally experienced obstacle. Include all 3528 consenting adult records with complete selected predictors and outcome. Geographic names are the supplied sample labels. Recruitment, residence verification and the actual language versions could not be verified from available documentation. No claim of population representativeness is made.

Predictors and source audit
Use recorded age in years, sex, education, seven separate binary employment indicators, AI-use frequencyQ 15 and four distinct itemsQ 18-Q 21. The reduced logistic model omitsQ 18-Q 21. For the original models, sex and education are one-hot encoded; other variables are numeric and standardised using training data only. The CatBoost extension uses the same information with native categorical handling as specified below. Q 17 is excluded: code 99, labelled a justified missing value by the provider, occurs exactly in the 395 never-users. The source questionnaire does not document the routing rule. Q 16,Q 22,Q 24, site, identifier, timing and locality-size variables are not predictors. The source questionnaire includesQ 14, but no separateQ 14 variable was delivered; it is not analysed or imputed. For the quality sensitivity use the thirteen available variablesQ 1-Q 13. Original records and codes remain unchanged.

Original models and validation
Multinomial L 2 logistic regression uses C={0.01,0.1,1,10}; RF uses 200 trees, square-root feature subsampling and minimum leaf size={10,30}; histogram gradient boosting uses 100 iterations, learning rate 0.05, minimum leaf size 30, L 2=10, maximum leaves={7,15}, with early stopping disabled. No class weighting or resampling is used. The original model seed is 20261014. Leave one supplied site out in each outer fold, with inner leave-one-training-site-out tuning by equal-site mean log loss. The random comparator uses five folds stratified by site and outcome, fixed split seed 20261014, and the same inner site-blocked tuning. All preprocessing and tuning use training data only.

Temperature scaling
Fit scalarT from inner out-of-fold probabilities, clipping probabilities at 1e-15 and optimising log(T) by scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar(method='bounded') over log(0.25) to log(4). The objective is equally weighted across training sites. Report raw and calibrated outer-test predictions; temperature preserves the most probable label.

Scoring and uncertainty
Primary scoring is equal-site mean log loss. Also report pooled and per-site log loss, multiclass Brier score, accuracy, macro-F 1, macro-recall, category recall and top-label ECE with ten fixed-width bins. The 2000 paired within-site respondent bootstrap resamples operate on fixed predictions and do not incorporate retraining or new-site sampling uncertainty. No significance-based selection among model families is undertaken.

Original sensitivities
Refit logistic regression after filtering duration<3 minutes or exact straightlining overQ 1-Q 13, omittingQ 21, and treating the five AI frequency/attitude predictors categorically. The quality rule is a deliberately simple sensitivity flag, not a validated definition of invalid responding.

Revision analyses
1. Compare the respondent-weighted add-one training prior with the arithmetic mean of separately add-one-smoothed training-site distributions; also calculate an unsmoothed equal-site sensitivity. No held-out outcome counts enter any prior.
2. Report every model's gain over its corresponding prior in both validation designs. The identity model-loss difference=prior-loss difference+difference in model gains is descriptive arithmetic, not causal identification of predictor-outcome shift.
3. Repeat RF LOSO with ten model seeds 20261014-20261023, keeping partitions fixed and repeating tuning and temperature estimation. Summarise metrics across fits without selecting a seed or averaging probabilities. These ranges quantify model randomness, not population uncertainty.
4. Extend the quality-screen and no-concern sensitivities to RF using the original model seed. The quality screen changes the test sample as well as the training sample.
5. For full LR and RF, match each outer training set to 2670 observations through five repeated draws stratified by site and category, using seeds 20261028-20261032 and fixed model/split seeds 20261014. Keep original test partitions and use shared training subsamples across models. Repeat preprocessing, inner tuning and calibration. Report each repetition's metrics and gains, not ensemble predictions. This matches outer training size but leaves differences in site composition, number of training sites and inner sample sizes.

Supplementary CatBoost comparison
The additional CatBoost analysis was specified before its first fit during revision, after the original model results were known. It is exploratory and not an independently preregistered or externally confirmed model comparison. It retains all 3528 records, all ten outcome categories and the same fifteen full-model predictors. It reproduces the six original outer site holdouts and five original random folds, and uses the same inner leave-one-training-site-out selection by equal-site mean log loss.

CatBoost 1.2.8 uses CPU Ordered boosting, learning rate 0.05 and the fixed eight-candidate grid iterations={100,500} x depth={4,6} x l2_leaf_reg={3,10}. The model seed and random-fold split seed are 20261014. Each fitted model uses one thread; four outer folds may run concurrently. Sex and education codes are supplied as strings and designated categorical. Age, the seven employment indicators, AI-use frequency and the four attitude items remain numeric. No external scaling, full-data target encoding or shared quantised Pool is used. All CatBoost transformations are learned from the relevant training subset. Site, target, identifiers and all other excluded columns are absent from the feature matrix.

No external evaluation set is supplied to fitting. Early stopping, best-iteration selection and class balancing are disabled. Other fixed options and resolved fitted parameters are recorded in the extension outputs. For each outer fold, select the candidate with lowest equal-site inner log loss, fit temperature to that candidate's inner out-of-fold probabilities using the original optimizer and bounds, and refit the selected model on the full outer training data. Report both raw and temperature-scaled predictions. Recompute respondent-weighted and equal-site add-one priors from the corresponding training data. Report every result, regardless of whether it improves on the original models.

The extension reports per-site, pooled and equal-site metrics, class-specific recall and confusion matrices, design-specific gains and the descriptive arithmetic loss-gap comparison. This extension uses one model seed. The ten-seed, matched-training-size, response-quality and concern-item sensitivity analyses remain scoped to the originally specified model families; they are not repeated for CatBoost. New population-level validation, recruitment evidence or measurement-equivalence evidence is not supplied by this additional algorithm.

catboost_extension.py check validates the fixed data structure and reproduces the original prior metrics without fitting. catboost_extension.py run freezes a hash-identified protocol before fitting, writes guarded atomic checkpoints and assembles the outputs. Public aggregates and configurations are written to results/revision/catboost/. Individual prediction arrays and test indices stay in private/catboost/ and are excluded from the public archive. Existing original model outputs are not overwritten. Code, data, version or protocol changes prevent an incompatible checkpoint resume.

Interpretation
The analyses quantify limited individual-response predictability and the smaller additional difference between represented-site and held-out-site evaluation in these samples. They do not identify cultural or geographic causes, establish measurement equivalence, validate intervention assignment or demonstrate accurate population prevalence estimation from model predictions.
