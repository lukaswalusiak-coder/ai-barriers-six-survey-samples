Analysis specification fixed before the first model fit on 14 October 2026

Exploratory secondary analysis, not preregistered. Primary outcome: the original ten-category answer to Q23 (greatest perceived barrier to wider AI use), including no need and hard to say. Retain all consenting adult records with valid predictors and outcome. Do not infer individual experienced barriers or actual future adoption.
Primary predictors: exact age, reported sex, education, seven employment indicators, AI-use frequency (Q15), anticipated need (Q18), benefit-risk appraisal (Q19), everyday-task trust (Q20), and societal concern (Q21). Q17 is excluded because it is structurally unavailable to never-users. Q16, Q22 and Q24 are excluded. Geographic labels define splits and are not predictors. Demographic comparator omits Q18-Q21.
Primary evaluation: outer leave-one-metropolitan-sample-out; all preprocessing, hyperparameter selection and temperature fitting confined to the other five samples. Inner leave-one-sample-out chooses the lowest equal-site mean log loss. Report all prespecified model families, not just the winner.
Models: smoothed empirical class prior; L2 multinomial logistic regression C={0.01,0.1,1,10}; random forest 200 trees with minimum leaf size={10,30}; histogram gradient boosting 100 iterations, learning rate=0.05, maximum leaf nodes={7,15}, minimum leaf size=30, L2=10, no early stopping. No class resampling or class weights in primary probabilistic models.
Probability calibration: one scalar temperature from inner out-of-sample predictions for the selected configuration, weighted equally by sample. Evaluate uncalibrated and calibrated versions on untouched outer samples. This is a nested calibration workflow, not evidence that calibration transfers universally.
Comparator: fivefold random respondent cross-validation stratified jointly by sample and outcome; nested site-blocked tuning retained. Different training sizes limit causal attribution of performance differences solely to shift.
Primary metric: equal-site mean multiclass log loss. Also respondent-pooled log loss, multiclass Brier score (sum across 10 classes), macro F1, macro recall, accuracy and top-label ECE with 10 fixed-width bins; report each held-out sample and each class.
Uncertainty: 2000 paired within-site respondent bootstrap resamples of fixed held-out predictions for equal-site log-loss differences. Intervals are conditional on fitted models and these six samples, not population or new-city intervals. No significance-based model selection.
Sensitivity: exclude duration<3 minutes or exact straightlining across Q1-Q13; remove Q21 to examine semantic proximity to the privacy response; treat ordinal AI predictors categorically. Refit logistic workflow only. No outcome regrouping based on predictive results.
Interpretation: emphasize limits of transportability, class imbalance and sampling. Do not report causal effects, cultural explanations or deployment readiness from predictive association.
